Research framework

A recurring workflow across my projects.

The specific system changes, but the research logic remains consistent.

  1. Understand the physical system
    Define governing phenomena, constraints, measurable outputs and engineering trade-offs.
  2. Generate or acquire high-quality data
    Use experiments, CFD or parametric simulations with structured DOE.
  3. Build predictive models
    Compare data-driven baselines with physics-guided or operator-learning methods.
  4. Optimize and generalize
    Explore design spaces, uncertainty, operating conditions and geometry variation.
  5. Move toward digital twins
    Connect models with real systems for rapid prediction, monitoring and decision support.
