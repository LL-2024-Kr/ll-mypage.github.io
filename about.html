Biography

About me

I am a Ph.D. researcher at Kongju National University in South Korea. My background spans PEM fuel-cell experiments, computational fluid dynamics, data-driven modeling and physics-informed machine learning.

My current research aims to connect simulation, experiments and AI into generalizable workflows for transport prediction, design optimization and digital twins. I am also expanding into intelligent robotics and AI-driven engineering systems.

Long term, I am interested in research and teaching at the intersection of energy systems, computational engineering and artificial intelligence.
